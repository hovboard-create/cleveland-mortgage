Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you close?

We can close in as little as 2 weeks. Many of our customers close in 7-10 days. You choose the timeline that works best for you.

Do you buy homes in any condition?

Yes! We buy homes in any condition — whether they need cosmetic updates or major repairs. You don't need to do any work before selling to us.

Will I get a fair price?

We base our offers on current market value, condition, location, and comparable sales. Our process is transparent — no surprise deductions or hidden fees.

Do I have to pay realtor commissions?

No. We cover all closing costs and realtor fees. You keep more of your money from the sale.

What if I have a mortgage?

No problem. We can pay off your mortgage from the sale proceeds. You walk away with the net proceeds after your loan is satisfied.

Do you do inspections and appraisals?

No appraisals needed. We may do a brief walkthrough to assess the property, but there are no formal inspections or appraisals. You won't have any surprise repairs required.
